My favorite cars

The cars I'd drive, own, or just stare at.

Ferrari Daytona SP3

Absolutely phenomenal car. It just looks so good. In my opinion this is the modern LaFerrari.

McLaren P1 HDK

This car is so good-looking it's insane. I've sat in one. Not comfy, but the style is unmatched.

Koenigsegg CC850

You can drive it as an automatic or as a manual. How freaking cool is that? I love driving manual cars.

Porsche 911 GT3 RS 4.0 (997)

The 4.0 is super rare. Only 600 were made, and every one is a manual, which is exactly how I'd want it. I rode in an RS 3.8 once and even that was an amazing car.

2026 Aston Martin Vanquish

An absolute monster of a car. V12 up front, all the power to the rear wheels. This is the car you drive to the country club.

2018 BMW M4 CS

BMWs have been trending hard online lately, which kind of annoys me because I like liking niche cars. But this car is just amazing in my eyes. My family loves BMWs too, and I've been dreaming about this one since I was 10.
